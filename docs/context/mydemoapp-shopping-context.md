## Feature

A shopper browses a catalogue, opens a product, adds it to the cart, signs in, enters a shipping address and payment details, reviews the order and places it.

## Screens in scope

Catalogue → Sort sheet → Product details → Cart → Login → Checkout: address → Checkout: payment → Checkout: review → Checkout: complete. Also the menu (for Log In / Log Out) and the empty cart. Out of scope: WebView, QR Code Scanner, Geo Location, Drawing, FingerPrint, Virtual USB, Crash app.

## Main journey

1. The catalogue shows products with a name and price.
2. Tap a product image: its details open with quantity 1.
3. Tap "Add to cart": the cart badge shows 1.
4. Open the cart: the product, its price, "1 Items" and the total are shown.
5. "Proceed To Checkout" while signed out opens Login.
6. Sign in as the demo user: the address form opens.
7. Fill the required address fields, "To Payment"; fill the card details, "Review Order".
8. The review shows the items, address, card holder and total; "Place Order".
9. "Checkout Complete" is shown; "Continue Shopping" returns to the catalogue, and the cart is empty.

## Edge cases

- Quantity at its lowest value (− at 1).
- Several different products in the cart; the same product added twice.
- Sign-in from the menu versus sign-in forced by checkout.
- App sent to the background and resumed in the middle of checkout.
